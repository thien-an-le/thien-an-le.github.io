About

Ammonia is an easy way to ship hydrogen. Getting the hydrogen back out, at a temperature and a catalyst cost a plant will accept, is the hard part. That's what I worked on at KRICT (Korea Research Institute of Chemical Technology) from 2019 to 2026.

On ruthenium, the bottleneck is two nitrogen atoms finding each other and leaving the surface. Most of my work is about speeding that up: which ceria facet the Ru sits on, putting nitrogen or lanthanum into the support, adding cesium. Every catalyst gets the same treatment, characterization (TPR, TPD, XPS, STEM, synchrotron XAS, in situ DRIFTS) read against kinetics, so I can say why it works and not only that it does.

A powder that looks great in a lab reactor can still fail in a plant. So the later work moved to 3 mm beads, less Ru, runs of 100 to 150 hours, and pilot fixed-bed tests. The solvothermal Ru/CeO2 catalyst made it the whole way and was licensed to Lotte Chemical.

Before KRICT I did my Ph.D. at Ajou University with Prof. Eun Duck Park, on nickel and cobalt catalysts for CO and CO2 methanation. That work produced 10 first-author papers and a granted patent, and it was licensed to CES Co., Ltd. Before that came an M.E. at Kyung Hee University (bio-oil from seaweed) and a B.E. at the Industrial University of Ho Chi Minh City. I work in English and Vietnamese.

Work

Ru catalysts for low-temperature ammonia cracking

KRICT, Hydrogen & C1 Gas Research Center · 2019–2026

Part of the national program "Integrated Original Technology for Use in Hydrogen Production and Storage".

  • Supports engineered around CeO2: {100} facets, La2O3–CeO2 composites, N-doped CeO2, with Cs as promoter.
  • Solvothermal, alcohol-assisted Ru/CeO2: 1,319.6 mmol H2 min⁻¹ gRu⁻¹ at 450 °C, stable for 100 h. Covered by US 12,251,684 B2.
  • Taken from bench to pilot and licensed to Lotte Chemical Corporation.

Cheaper metals, and shapes a reactor can use

KRICT · 2022–2026

  • Ni/Al–Ce oxides made in one pot by cation–anion double hydrolysis: full NH3 conversion near 550 °C, no Ru.
  • Ce-modified Co–Fe layered double oxides: 81.9% conversion at 450 °C, stable 80 h at 550 °C.
  • 3 mm Ru/Al2O3 beads with a K-promoted La–Ce shell: about 80% conversion near 500 °C for 150 h in a bench reactor.

Ni and Co catalysts for CO and CO2 methanation

Ajou University, Ph.D. with Prof. Eun Duck Park · 2015–2019

Program "Novel Catalyst Technology for C1 Gas Activation". I varied the support (Al2O3 phases, SiO2, SiC, CeO2, core–shell Al@Al2O3 and Al@MAl2O4 spinels), the promoter and the preparation route. Ten first-author papers; the results backed a granted patent and the license to CES Co., Ltd.

Bio-oil from seaweed

Kyung Hee University, M.E. · 2012–2014

Catalytic pyrolysis of Saccharina japonica and CoP/Al2O3 catalysts for hydrodeoxygenation of the oil. Two first-author papers.
